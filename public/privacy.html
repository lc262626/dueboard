Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What DueBoard does

DueBoard shows your assignments from Google Classroom, Moodle, Canvas and Blackboard in one list and calendar. It only reads due dates and assignment details. It never changes, submits or deletes anything in your accounts.

What it can see

  • Google Classroom: the names of your classes and your coursework (titles, due dates, links, and whether you turned it in). Access is read-only.
  • Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard: the events in the calendar link you paste in.

What it stores

  • While you're connected, the server keeps your Google access key or your calendar links so it can load your assignments. These are tied only to your browser's login cookie.
  • If you log in to Moodle with a username and password, the password is used once to get an access key and is never saved.
  • Checkmarks, your calendar view and game scores are saved only in your own browser.

What it never does

  • It doesn't sell, share or show your information to anyone else. Other people using DueBoard can't see your assignments.
  • It doesn't use your information for ads or for training anything.

Removing your information

Click Disconnect on any account to delete what DueBoard stores for it. You can also remove DueBoard's access to Google at any time from your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps.

Other services

The hidden music feature plays a video from YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.
